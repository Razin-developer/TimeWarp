## 🎮 CONTROLS BREAKDOWN:

### 1. opening the sidepanel
go to any site like youtube.com or wikipedia.org. click the puzzle piece icon in chrome top right, click **Time Warp**, and the panel slides out from the right!

### 2. timeline slider
drag the slider at the bottom to travel back to 2008! if u hover over the track, it speculatively pre-loads snapshots in background so its instant when u click.

### 3. autoplay mode
click the Play button! it automatically jumps month-by-month through history like a movie clip while u eat snacks.

### 4. diff inspector tool
click the `#` or grid button to open split view! red = deleted code, green = new code. use the **MINIMAP** on the right side to jump straight to changes!

### 5. bookmarks & tags
click the star icon to save epic dates with tags like `#retro` or `#2012vibe`.

### 6. qr code generator
click the QR button to show a QR code! scan it with ur phone camera to view the old web page on mobile! 📲
