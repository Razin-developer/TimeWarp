# how to use time warp (user guide)

hey so if you loaded the extension but don't know what buttons to press, read this page. i made a lot of buttons so listen carefully.

## 1. open the sidepanel
first go to any website, like `wikipedia.org` or `google.com`.
click the extension puzzle piece icon in chrome, find **Time Warp**, and click it. 
the sidepanel will slide open on the right side of your screen.

## 2. the timeline slider
at the bottom of the sidepanel there is a slider. 
- drag the slider to change the date! you can go back all the way to 2008!
- if you hover your mouse over the slider track, it shows a little tooltip with the date BEFORE you click it. this is speculatively loading the pages in the background so it is very fast.
- if you move your mouse away, it cancels the download so your internet doesn't lag.

## 3. autoplay (month-by-month)
if you don't wanna drag the slider manually, click the play button next to the arrow keys. 
it will automatically play like a movie, but instead of showing every single day (which is slow and annoying), it jumps month-by-month! press pause to stop it.

## 4. diff inspector (the comparison tool)
click the `#` button or the diff inspector button (looks like a grid) to compare the live page with the past.
- it will open a new tab with a split view.
- red is stuff that got deleted, green is new stuff.
- on the right side, there is a **MINIMAP**. you can click on the colored lines in the minimap to scroll directly to the changes!
- you can search for keywords in the search bar, or turn on dark mode with the moon button.

## 5. bookmarking and tags
if you find a cool year, click the star icon in the sidepanel.
it will ask you to enter a tag (like "cool layout" or "retro").
you can see all your bookmarks in the main dashboard page. you can search by tag using `#tag-name` (like `#retro`).

## 6. sharing qr code
if you wanna show your friend a page on your phone, click the QR code button in the sidepanel.
it will open a new tab with a QR code. scan it with your phone and you can look at the wayback page there!
